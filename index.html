I’m a product manager with an engineer’s understanding of the machinery and an economist’s interest in the tradeoffs.

At Lucidworks, I own product direction across enterprise AI and platform experiences, including the core UI and usability experience. My work connects customer discovery, product strategy, hands-on prototyping, and the decisions that get a team moving.

I came to product through search engineering, working directly with enterprise and government customers. That experience still shapes how I work: understand the actual problem, get close to the details, and make something useful.
