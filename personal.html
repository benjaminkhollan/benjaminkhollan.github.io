Outside of work

When I'm not working or exploring new personal interests, I love spending time outdoors with friends. Up until 2021, I competed in running at a relatively high level, but since finishing collegiate athletics, my range of hobbies has expanded. While I still run a few miles each week, I've added climbing, skiing, hiking, and recently, biking to my routine. Outside of outdoor activities, I also enjoy reading, listening to music and podcasts, and playing cribbage.
